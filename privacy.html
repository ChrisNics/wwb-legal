Terms of Service

Why We Believed Publisher (“the app”) is a private tool used by the creator of Why We Believed, a history and philosophy channel, to upload videos the creator made to the creator’s own social media accounts, including TikTok and a Facebook Page.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in with it, and only to accounts the owner controls.

Content

Every video posted through the app is original work by Why We Believed. Archival images used in the videos are public domain or used under their licence, with credits in each post’s caption. Posts follow TikTok’s Community Guidelines and Meta’s Community Standards.

Third-party platforms

Use of TikTok and Facebook through the app is also governed by those platforms’ own terms. The app does not change, bypass or automate anything beyond uploading and publishing the owner’s videos and captions.

No warranty

The app is provided as is. The owner may change or stop it at any time and will update this page when these terms change.

Privacy Policy

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the signed-in TikTok account (display name and account ID), to confirm which account is posting.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account, and to the owner’s Facebook Page.

What it stores

Access tokens are stored only on the owner’s own computer, in a local file that is never shared or uploaded elsewhere. The app does not run a server and keeps no database.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok or Facebook users.
  • It does not sell, share or use any data for advertising or analytics.
  • It does not read messages, comments or contacts.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or Facebook (Settings → Business integrations). Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless; the owner then deletes them.

Contact

Questions about this app can be sent as a message to the Why We Believed Facebook Page.
